# Northwind Heating & Air — Pricing

Every dollar amount Northwind will quote over the phone appears on this page. There are
no other approved figures.

## Diagnostic / service call fee

| When | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Standard hours (Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm) | **$89** |
| After hours (evenings, weekends, holidays) | **$149** |
| Comfort Plan members, any time | **Waived** |

The diagnostic fee covers travel and the visit. It is credited toward the repair if the
customer approves the work the same visit.

## Travel fee

**$45** for addresses in the extended service area. Waived for Comfort Plan members.

## Common repairs — parts and labor

| Repair | Typical range |
| --- | --- |
| Capacitor replacement | $150 – $300 |
| Ignitor or flame sensor | $200 – $400 |
| Blower motor | $450 – $900 |
| Heat exchanger | $1,200 – $3,000 |
| AC refrigerant leak repair | $400 – $1,500 |
| Water heater replacement | $1,400 – $2,800 |
| Drain clearing | $175 – $400 |
| Toilet or faucet repair | $150 – $450 |

These are ranges, not quotes. The technician confirms the price on site before doing any
work, and no work begins without the customer's approval.

## Comfort Plan

**$19 per month.** Includes waived diagnostic and travel fees, two seasonal tune-ups per
year, 15% off all repairs, and priority scheduling ahead of non-members.

## Quoting rules

Quote the range as written. Do not narrow it, average it, or estimate a specific number
inside it — "somewhere around six hundred" is not on this page.

If a caller asks about anything not listed, say the technician will confirm the price on
site. Never estimate. An invented figure a customer repeats back three days later is
worse than no figure at all.
