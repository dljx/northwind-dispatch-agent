# Northwind Heating & Air — Policies

## Hours

Standard hours are Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. After-hours calls are answered around
the clock and dispatched to the on-call technician.

## What qualifies as an emergency

An emergency gets the next available priority slot and pages the on-call technician
immediately.

- No heat when the outdoor temperature is below 40°F
- No cooling when the outdoor temperature is above 90°F
- An active water leak that cannot be shut off at a valve
- Sewage backing up into the home
- No hot water in a home with no working alternative

## What does not qualify

These are booked as same-day or routine, and the caller should be told so directly rather
than left expecting a truck tonight.

- Seasonal maintenance and tune-ups
- A unit that is noisy, smells dusty on first startup, or cycles oddly but still runs
- One slow drain where other fixtures drain normally
- Thermostat programming and settings questions
- Estimates for planned replacement

## Brands serviced

Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Bryant, Goodman, American Standard, and Mitsubishi
ductless systems.

Northwind does not service geothermal systems, boilers over 25 years old, or commercial
rooftop units. For those, say Northwind is not the right fit and end the call politely
rather than booking a visit that will be turned away at the door.

## Warranty

One year on Northwind labor. Manufacturer parts warranties are passed through at their
full term. Drain clearing carries a 90-day warranty on the same line.

## Payment

Payment is due on completion. Northwind accepts card, check, and ACH. No deposit is taken
to book an appointment, and no payment details are ever collected over the phone.

## Cancellations

No charge to cancel or reschedule with two hours' notice. Inside two hours, the
diagnostic fee applies.

## What to hand to a human

Transfer to the on-call human, rather than continuing, when the caller:

- asks for a manager, a complaint, or a refund
- is disputing a previous invoice or a previous technician's work
- has a question about warranty coverage on a specific past job
- is clearly frustrated, or has asked the same thing twice without getting what they need

Transferring early is cheaper than transferring after a caller has repeated themselves.
