# Northwind Heating & Air — Service Area

Northwind serves the Minneapolis–St. Paul metro from a single dispatch yard. Twelve
trucks, no franchises, no subcontracted work.

## Core service area — no travel fee

Minneapolis: 55401, 55403, 55404, 55405, 55406, 55407, 55408, 55409, 55410, 55411,
55412, 55413, 55414, 55416, 55417, 55418, 55419

Inner suburbs: Edina 55424 and 55435, St. Louis Park 55426, Richfield 55423,
Bloomington 55420 and 55431, Golden Valley 55427, Roseville 55113

## Extended service area — $45 travel fee

Minnetonka 55305 and 55345, Eden Prairie 55344 and 55347, Plymouth 55441 and 55447,
Maple Grove 55369, Brooklyn Park 55443, Eagan 55122, Woodbury 55125, Maplewood 55109

The travel fee is charged once per visit, not per hour, and is waived for Comfort Plan
members.

## Outside the service area

Anything not listed above. Northwind does not travel beyond the extended area, including
for emergencies.

If a caller is outside the area, say so plainly and early — before discussing the problem
in any detail, and before quoting anything. Do not offer to check, do not offer a
callback, and do not book. Wasting a caller's time and then declining is worse than
declining immediately.

Suggested wording: "We don't cover that area, unfortunately. I'd hate to have you wait on
a truck that isn't coming."

## Address confirmation

Always confirm the full service address out loud before booking, even for a known
customer calling about a previously serviced property. People move, own rentals, and call
about their parents' houses.
